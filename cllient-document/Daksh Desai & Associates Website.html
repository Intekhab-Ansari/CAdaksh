    Left out on purpose

    Content on the reference sites that the ICAI Website and Advertisement Guidelines restrict.

    • "Why choose us" sections (both sites), and claims such as "zero missed deadlines", "15+ years", "24–48 hour turnaround", "trusted by founders, SMEs and NRIs" (RSC)
    • SEO keywords such as "Top CA firm in India" and "Best CA near me" in KM's meta tags
    • Slogans and taglines: "Brains that lead…" and the per-service taglines (RSC)
    • Personal awards, ranks, scholarships and felicitations (KM team page)
    • The ICAI AI certification in RSC's timeline
    • RSC's "AI Solutions" service line (office automation is not a CA professional service)
    • "Financing assistance" (RSC): ICAI bars "arrangement of loans"; capital structuring advice is kept
    • Newsletter sign-up (KM): pushing information about exclusive CA services is not allowed
    • Floating WhatsApp chat button (KM); the number is listed on Contact instead
    • Stock photos of people; only passport-style photos are allowed
    • Long statutory summaries, section numbers, form tables and penalty tables (KM), as asked
    • Other occupations or associations (LLB studies, the CJS Legal retainer)
